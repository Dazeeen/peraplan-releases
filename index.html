Budget planner para sa inyong dalawa — pati sa pamilya at barkada.

PeraPlan helps couples, families and friends plan money together: income and paydays, expenses, bills, budget limits and savings goals — in Tagalog or English.

Download for AndroidAndroid 13 and up

Installing / Pag-install

  1. Android 13 o mas bago ang kailangan. Tingnan sa Settings → About phone → Android version. Sa Android 12 pababa, lalabas ang "There was a problem parsing the package" — hindi ito gagana doon.
  2. I-download gamit ang Chrome at hintaying matapos (mga 50–60 MB). Huwag i-forward ang APK sa Messenger o ibang chat app — madalas nasisira o napuputol ang file, kaya "parse error".
  3. Buksan ang file mula sa Downloads, at payagan ang "Install unknown apps" para sa Chrome kapag tinanong.
  4. Kung may PeraPlan na: i-update lang mula sa app (Iba pa → Tingnan kung may update). Hindi nabubura ang data.

Needs Android 13 or newer. Download with Chrome and let it finish; files forwarded through chat apps often arrive broken and show "parse error".

No server of our own

Your plans are saved on your phone. Phones in a plan sync through encrypted messages, and the key exists only inside the plan's invite code.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you sign in with Google, PeraPlan saves your plans in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so you can get them back on a new phone. PeraPlan can't see any other files in your Drive.
